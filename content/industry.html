01 · Medical clinics

Be the clinic that's easy to find, and easy to book.

Clinic patients usually have a specific need and a short window. They search locally, compare a few options, and book whoever answers first.

What gets in the way

  • Service pages that don't match what people search for, so you're missing from local results.
  • Calls and web forms that wait for a free moment at the front desk.
  • No clear view of which marketing is producing appointments.

How we can help

  • Local SEO and service pages built around real patient searches.
  • Treatment-based Google Ads pointing to focused landing pages.
  • Instant follow-up by text and email, with a CRM and one dashboard.

02 · Med spas & aesthetic clinics

Turn treatment searches into a fuller calendar.

Aesthetic patients shop by treatment, such as microneedling, injectables or laser, and often enquire with several spas at once. Speed and clarity win the booking.

What gets in the way

  • Generic ads and a homepage where patients wanted a page about one treatment.
  • Enquiries that arrive after hours and go cold by morning.
  • Past patients who are due for their next treatment but never hear from you.

How we can help

  • Treatment-level campaigns with a landing page for each offer.
  • 24/7 capture and follow-up, plus a structured 14-day nurture sequence.
  • Reactivation of past patients based on treatment cycles.

03 · Dental practices

Keep the chairs full, from the first enquiry.

Dental patients range from routine check-ups to considered treatments like implants and aligners. Each needs different messaging and a different follow-up pace.

What gets in the way

  • One generic message for every treatment, from cleanings to full-arch work.
  • Phone-first booking that misses evening and weekend enquiries.
  • New practices with no patient base and no room for dropped leads.

How we can help

  • Treatment-specific ads and pages for high-value and routine care.
  • Automated response and 14-day follow-up for every enquiry.
  • SEO, AEO and GEO so you appear in search and AI answers.

04 · Plastic surgeons

Be recommended, and remembered, through a long decision.

Surgical patients research carefully, compare surgeons, and increasingly ask AI assistants for recommendations. The practice that's visible, credible and patient wins the consultation.

What gets in the way

  • Expertise that doesn't come through in search results or AI answers.
  • High-value consultation enquiries that get one reply, then silence.
  • Advertising that has to respect stricter rules for medical procedures.

How we can help

  • AEO and GEO: structured, answer-ready content about procedures and your practice.
  • Procedure-focused campaigns that inform rather than overpromise.
  • Long-cycle nurture that keeps you present until the patient is ready.

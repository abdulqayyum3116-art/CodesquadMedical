The challenge

How CareInn Dental Clinic Grew Patient Volume From Day One

CareInn Dental Clinic launched with no existing patient pipeline. There was no history of leads, no established follow-up habits, and no base of returning patients to build on.

Every new patient had to come from somewhere — website enquiries, phone calls, and referrals — but without a system in place from day one, there was a real risk of losing early momentum before the clinic had a chance to build a reputation.

A new clinic can't afford slow response times or dropped leads. Every enquiry in those first months matters more, because there's no existing patient base to fall back on.

CareInn needed a growth system in place before the doors opened, not something bolted on after gaps in the process had already cost them patients.

The solution

A connected system designed around the way the team actually works.

CodeSquad built CareInn's patient growth system from the ground up, live before the clinic's first day of operation. The system captures every lead, responds within 60 seconds, follows up automatically over 14 days, and gives the team full visibility — all live from day one, with no existing process to work around.

Stack: Website + Make.com + Airtable + SMS + Email + custom dashboard.

Conclusion

CareInn Dental Clinic didn't have to learn the hard way what happens when there's no system in place for new patient enquiries — the growth system was already running before their first patient walked through the door.

Since launch, every enquiry has gone through the same connected pipeline: captured, answered, followed up, and tracked in one place.

That's the difference between building a growth system to fix a broken process and building one so there was never a gap to begin with — and it's why patient volume at CareInn has been growing since day one.

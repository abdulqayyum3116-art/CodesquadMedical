The challenge

How RTM Kinetics Connected Patient Care From Lead to Recovery

RTM Kinetics needed to manage the full patient relationship — from the first enquiry through active rehabilitation — but that relationship lived across disconnected tools.

Patient leads, contact history, and reconnect campaigns for patients who'd gone quiet were tracked separately from the exercise and recovery data therapists needed to do their jobs.

Patient feedback on their care was collected inconsistently, if at all, making it hard to spot problems early or show the impact of a program.

Without a connected system, the team was spending time stitching together patient context by hand instead of focusing on care.

The solution

A connected system designed around the way the team actually works.

CodeSquad built RTM Kinetics a connected system spanning the full patient lifecycle, from lead to recovery.

The system manages the patient CRM end to end — capturing leads, automating reconnect campaigns for patients who've gone quiet, and collecting structured patient feedback — while syncing with the platform's remote monitoring tools so therapists have one place to see the full picture.

Stack: React/Node.js + PostgreSQL + SMS/Email automation + remote monitoring integration.

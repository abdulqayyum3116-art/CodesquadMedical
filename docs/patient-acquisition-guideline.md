## 7. Sample messaging

Show this as a phone-style SMS mockup and an email card, with a timeline of the 14-day sequence. Label it "Sample messaging". Keep {placeholders} visible so it reads as a template.

### Instant response (sent within 3 seconds of form submission)

**SMS:** Hi [First Name], thanks for contacting {Clinic Name}! Our team will reach out to you soon. Reserve your visit: {Booking Link} This month's offers: {Specials Link} Reply STOP to unsubscribe.

**Email — Subject:** Thanks for reaching out to {Clinic Name}, [First Name]!
Hi [First Name], we've received your inquiry and appreciate you thinking of {Clinic Name}. Someone from our team will reach out very soon. Want to skip the wait? Reserve your visit online: {Booking Link}. See what's on offer this month: {Specials Link}. Talk soon! — {Person Name}, {Clinic Name}

### 14-day re-engagement sequence (stops the moment a lead books or replies)

| Day | Channel | Goal | Sample (aesthetics) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | SMS | Personal check-in + booking nudge | Hey [First Name], {Person Name} with {Clinic Name} here. Wanted to make sure all your questions were answered. This {Month}, new clients get 20% off their first Signature Glow Facial plus a free skin consultation. Reserve: {Booking Link} |
| 3 | Email | What to expect, why us, featured offer | Subject: [First Name], here's what your first visit at {Clinic Name} looks like |
| 5 | SMS | Quick question + monthly offers | Any questions I can answer? Our {Month} offer is still open, plus specials on Microneedling and Laser Hair Removal. {Booking Link} |
| 8 | Email | First-visit reassurance + treatment highlights | Subject: First time at a med spa? Here's how it works. |
| 12 | SMS | Last gentle nudge | I don't want to keep bugging you, so this is my last message — our {Month} offers wrap up soon. Book whenever it suits you: {Booking Link} |

### Offer swaps per industry

Same sequence, different offer and email subjects:

| Industry | Featured offer | Day 8 email subject |
